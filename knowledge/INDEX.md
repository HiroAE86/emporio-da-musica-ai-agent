# Knowledge index

- `policies/store.md`: sections 1–10 of the store policy manual, including hours, payments, exchanges and returns, freight, promotions, WhatsApp service, warranty, privacy, and final rules.
- Product catalog: the supplied `*products.csv` file; categories are inferred from product names and descriptions. Supported category labels include violão, guitarra, baixo, bateria, teclado, piano, and ukulele.
- Orders: local `*orders.csv` and `*customers.csv` files. Order lookup stays local and returns only verified status and estimated delivery.

The source policy PDF and product/order CSVs remain the source of truth.
